Batamiga — Amiga 500 (OCS/ECS)

Put your Amiga 500 games here on the SHARE partition:
  /userdata/roms/amiga500/

Formats: .adf .adz .dms .ipf .hdf .lha .zip .m3u .uae .scp .raw
Multi-disk games: list the disks in a .m3u file (one per line).

Default emulator: libretro/puae (PUAE). Amiberry is also available:
in EmulationStation, open a game -> Options -> Emulator -> Amiberry.

Kickstart: copy a legally obtained Kickstart 1.3 (kick34005.A500) into
  /userdata/bios/amiga/
AROS (open source, no ROM needed) is bundled and works out of the box.
