Batamiga — Amiga CD32

Put your CD32 disc images here on the SHARE partition:
  /userdata/roms/amigacd32/

Formats: .iso .cue (+ .bin) .chd .nrg
One disc per game; use a .m3u for multi-disc titles.

Default emulator: libretro/puae (PUAE), CD32 model with Fast RAM.
Amiberry is available too: open a game -> Options -> Emulator -> Amiberry.

Kickstart: copy a legally obtained Kickstart 3.1 plus the CD32 extended
ROM into /userdata/bios/amiga/
  kick40060.CD32
  kick40060.CD32.ext
The bundled AROS ROM does NOT cover CD32 — a real CD32 Kickstart is
required for this system.

Controller: a 7-button CD32 pad is emulated on port 1 by default.
